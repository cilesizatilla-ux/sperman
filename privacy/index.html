SPERMAN Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · applies to version 1.1 and later

In short: Offline play (story, bots, same-screen) sends nothing to us. Online features (friends, invites, online matches) are for ages 13 and up and need an online account: a nickname and a random player ID that we keep with our service provider Unity in the USA, after you give your explicit consent in the game. Rewarded ads are optional, are shown by Google AdMob and are not personalized. We do not track you and we do not sell data.

1. Who we are

SPERMAN: The Egg Hunt is developed by Atilla Cilesiz ("we"), an individual developer in Türkiye. We are the data controller (under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, "KVKK", and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR") for the data described below. This policy applies to the iPhone version 1.1 and later. Version 1.0 had no online features and no ads and collected nothing; its policy (effective October 17, 2026) is replaced by this one. You can reach us at cilesiz.atilla@gmail.com.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Game save: your name, character look, level, in-game currencies (including GEMS), items, mission and story progress, and settings (including your language choice).
  • Purchase record: so that each purchase grants GEMS only once, the App Store transaction number, the product, the amount of GEMS added and the date are written to the save.
  • Online account record: your nickname and number, your random player ID, whether you passed the age check (only "yes" or "no"; your birth year is never stored), your OPEN TO INVITES setting, and a saved copy of your friends list so that it can be shown without a connection.
  • None of this leaves your device except what is described in sections 3 to 6. Without an online account, offline play sends nothing to us or to Unity. The only other connections are to Apple (purchases) and, only if you choose to watch an ad, to Google (section 5).
  • The game does not use your local network, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

3. Online play: account, nickname, friends, invites and matches

  • Age check and consent: online features are for ages 13 and up. The game asks for your birth year and checks it on your device only; only "yes" or "no" is kept. If you are under 13, online features stay off on that device and every offline mode still works. Before the game connects to our service provider for the first time, it shows a notice and asks for your explicit consent. If you do not consent, nothing is sent and you can keep playing offline.
  • Online account: the game creates an anonymous account with Unity Authentication. It has no e-mail address and no password: it consists of a random player ID and the nickname you choose, to which the service adds a five-digit number (for example KAPTAN#48213). Your nickname is the name other players see. It is filtered (Turkish and English word lists, no phone numbers or contact details) and you can change it, which gives you a new number.
  • Friends: you add a friend with their nickname and number, or with an invite link that you or they share. The friend list, friend requests and your block list are stored by Unity's Friends service. A request needs the other person's approval. Nobody can browse or search for players by name alone.
  • Online status and invites (on by default): while the game is open, your friends can see whether you are online, away or busy, and game invites can reach you. Unity's Friends service also keeps the time you were last online; the game does not show it. An invite is a short note attached to the host's status that holds a room ID, the invited players' IDs, a random code and a time; it disappears when the room closes or the invite expires. You can turn this off in SETTINGS › ACCOUNT › OPEN TO INVITES. When it is off, friends see you as online only while your FRIENDS screen is open, you receive no invites and you cannot send any.
  • Online matches (CLASSIC ARENA 1v1 and LABYRINTH PARTY): the host's device opens a private room on Unity's Multiplayer services (Lobby and Relay). Players in the room see your nickname, hero and look. Match traffic (movement, hits, goals) is passed on in real time through Unity's relay, which hides your connection address from the other players; we do not store it. After a match you can add the other players as friends, report them or block them.
  • Invite links: the link that the SHARE button creates holds your player ID and nickname after a # sign. You decide who receives it. See "Our website and support e-mail" in section 9.
  • What is not collected online: there is no chat, voice or free text other than the filtered nickname. Item bonuses are switched off in online matches for everyone. Match results are not sent to a ranking.
  • IP address: every connection to Unity shows Unity your IP address, from which an approximate region can be derived. Unity keeps connection logs that include it for about 30 days.
  • Why we use this data: only to run the online features and keep them safe (accounts, friends, invites, matches, abuse prevention). We do not use it for advertising, profiling or analytics of our own.

4. Unity, our service provider in the USA

The online account, friends, lobby and relay run on Unity Gaming Services, operated by Unity Technologies SF, 116 New Montgomery Street, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA. Unity processes this data on our behalf under its Data Processing Addendum and terms; its privacy policy explains how Unity handles data. Your data is therefore transferred to the USA and may be processed elsewhere as Unity describes; Unity does not publish which country stores each service's data.

Basis of the transfer. We ask for your explicit consent in the game before the first connection, and we rely on that consent and on Unity's data processing terms. For people in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Unity's addendum includes standard contractual clauses and the UK and Swiss supplements. For Türkiye, KVKK Article 9 expects, for ongoing transfers, a standard contract in the form published by the Personal Data Protection Board. We asked Unity to sign it; on November 2, 2026 Unity told us that it cannot currently sign the Board's standard contract. So we do not have that contract, and we are telling you so openly. We continue to look for an additional safeguard. If you do not want your data transferred, do not create an online account: every offline mode works without one. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your online account (section 7).

5. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

  • What the ads are for: in the shop you can watch one ad to unlock one ULTRA item until you close the game. Ads are always optional and are shown only after you tap WATCH AD; there are no banner or other ads. The game shows an "About ads" card first, and Google's advertising software does not start until you accept it.
  • Provider: ads are served by Google through AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, the game asks Google for one. Google receives your IP address (from which it derives an approximate region), device and app details (such as device model, operating system and app version, and device identifiers used by its software), information about the ad (shown, completed, clicked) and crash and performance data. Google may use this to show, measure and secure ads. The game does not pass your nickname, player ID, friends or save to Google.
  • Not personalized: the game requests non-personalized ads only. They are chosen from the game's content, your approximate region and device type. Ad content is limited to the "PG" rating.
  • No tracking: the game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not use the advertising identifier to follow you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown where the law requires it before an ad can be requested. You can reopen it in SETTINGS › PRIVACY › AD CHOICES (shown where it applies) and withdraw your agreement to ads in SETTINGS › PRIVACY › ABOUT ADS › WITHDRAW; ads then stop until you accept again.
  • Report an ad: SETTINGS › PRIVACY › REPORT AN AD prepares an e-mail to us with the ad's ID so that we can report it to Google.
  • Google handles the data it collects under its own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Google's servers are outside Türkiye (see section 4 for the legal basis; here too we rely on your explicit consent when you accept the ads card).

6. In-app purchases

GEMS packs are sold through Apple's App Store; the game uses only Apple's system for purchases. Apple takes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Your purchase information is not sent to any company other than Apple. Apple's privacy policy explains how Apple handles this information. This has not changed from version 1.0.

7. Retention, deletion and your account

  • Account data (player ID, nickname, friends, requests, blocks, online status) is kept until you delete your online account. Unity keeps a player ID until it is deleted.
  • Connection logs at Unity, which include your IP address, are kept for about 30 days. Deleting your account does not remove log entries at once; they expire on their own after that period.
  • Delete in the game: SETTINGS › ACCOUNT › DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT (two steps). It deletes your friends, requests and blocks and then your nickname and player ID from Unity's service. Your progress, items and GEMS on your device stay. It cannot be undone, and you can withdraw your consent this way.
  • Delete by e-mail: if you can no longer open the game, write to cilesiz.atilla@gmail.com with your nickname and number; we may ask you to prove the account is yours before we delete it. Deleting the app does not delete the online account.
  • Other players' devices may keep your old nickname for a short time. Game save data stays on your device until you delete the app; device backups (for example iCloud Backup) may contain a copy.
  • Reports you send us by e-mail and our replies are kept only as long as needed to handle the case and any appeal, and for no more than 12 months after it is closed.

8. Community rules, reports and blocking

  • Nicknames are filtered. Offensive, insulting or impersonating names and behaviour are not allowed, and we may close accounts that break the rules.
  • Next to every other player's nickname you can report (offensive nickname, personal information, cheating, bad behavior, other) and block them. Blocking works at once on your device and ends the friendship; it also stops requests and invites from that player.
  • Reports reach us by e-mail at cilesiz.atilla@gmail.com. We aim to review them within 24 hours.

9. Our website and support e-mail

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any website host, GitHub sees the IP address and browser details of visitors. The pages use no cookies, analytics tools or external resources. On the invite page (/add/) the invite sits in the part of the address after the # sign, which your browser never sends to any server. E-mail you send us is handled in our Gmail mailbox (Google). We use what you write only to answer you.

10. Children

SPERMAN is rated 13+ in the App Store and is not directed at children. Online features are for ages 13 and up (section 3). The ULTRA ad feature is also meant for players 13 and older; if you are under 13, please do not watch ads. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, and if we learn that we did, we delete it. Parents can approve their children's purchases with Ask to Buy in Apple's Family Sharing.

11. Sharing, selling and tracking

We do not sell your data and we do not track you. We share data only with the providers named above, who act for us or, in Google's and Apple's case, under their own terms: Unity (online services), Google (ads, only if you watch one, and the support mailbox), Apple (purchases) and GitHub (website). We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data. Under KVKK Article 11 you can ask whether we process your data, ask for information about it, learn its purpose, learn which third parties in Türkiye or abroad receive it, ask for correction, ask for deletion or destruction, ask that these actions be notified to third parties, object to results against you from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage. Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Articles 15 to 22), and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Write to us at cilesiz.atilla@gmail.com with your nickname and number; we answer within 30 days (KVKK) or one month (GDPR), free of charge. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (kvkk.gov.tr) or to the data protection authority in your country.

13. Changes and version history

If we change this policy, we publish the new text on this page and update the effective date. We explain any new data collection here before the version that introduces it is released.

  • November 7, 2026 (version 1.1 and later): online accounts with nickname, friends, invites and online matches (Unity Gaming Services), rewarded ads (Google AdMob), 13+ check for online features.
  • October 17, 2026 (version 1.0): no online features, no ads, no data collected.
